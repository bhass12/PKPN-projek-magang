# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
General public, domestic and international travelers, and regional enthusiasts seeking comprehensive Indonesian destination guides, cultural insights, and travel recommendations across all provinces.

## Product Purpose
Provide an accessible, trustworthy, and richly curated national tourism directory and portal that showcases Indonesia's diverse destinations, provincial heritage, and regional insights, making discovery intuitive and culturally enriching.

## Positioning
A comprehensive national tourism directory and statistical open-data repository across Indonesia's provinces that bridges rich regional datasets (coordinates, categories, curated destinations) with institutional credibility and cultural depth, setting it apart from commercial booking platforms or superficial listicles.

## Operating Context
Accessed on desktop and mobile web browsers by visitors discovering destinations, planning regional journeys, exploring provincial tourism data, or accessing institutional programs and studies.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Comprehensive provincial directory structured by regions, capital cities, categories (nature, culture, beaches, religion, history), and featured attractions with coordinates.
- Integrated programmatic offerings: workshops, certified trainings, policy dialogues, and publications.
- Built on Laravel 12 with Blade templating, Vite, and modern CSS/Tailwind.
- High-volume local data source in `resources/data/provinsi.php`.

## Brand Commitments
- Name: PKPN (Pusat Kajian Pariwisata Nusantara).
- Tone & Voice: Authoritative yet inspiring, welcoming, culturally respectful, and clear.
- Core Identity: Deep oceanic navy, natural teal, and warm gold accents reflecting Indonesian maritime and cultural heritage.

## Evidence on Hand
- Curated provincial tourism dataset covering Indonesian provinces, capitals, regions, and featured destinations with coordinates in `resources/data/provinsi.php`.
- Established route structures and views across `resources/views/` (Beranda, Destinasi wisata, Tentang, Kajian, Program).
- Program curriculum, schedule, and pricing data mapped in `routes/web.php`.

## Product Principles
- **Regional Authenticity**: Celebrate the distinct character and cultural heritage of each province without generic homogenization.
- **Exploratory Clarity**: Make navigating large datasets (provinces, regions, categories) effortless, responsive, and visually intuitive.
- **Trustworthy Information**: Provide grounded, verified data—from coordinates to cultural context—that travelers and researchers can rely on.
