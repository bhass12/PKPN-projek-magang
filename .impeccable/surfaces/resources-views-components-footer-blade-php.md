## Direction contract

THESIS: The footer must close the visit like the last page of a well-edited publication — calm, credible, typographic — instead of arriving as a loud gradient-and-glass band.

OWN-WORLD: Flat deep-oceanic-navy ground (#0B1B2B), off-white text at reduced opacity, single natural-teal accent for links and marks, Epilogue display face + Be Vietnam Pro body, hairline borders, generous whitespace. No gradients, glass, glow, or gradient buttons.

STORY: Visitors leave with quiet confidence; the footer should feel like the end of authoritative, culturally grounded research rather than a generic closing template.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Brand column (logo, short positioning line, quiet socials) beside Menu Cepat and Area Kajian, with Hubungi Kami contact rows; centered typographic newsletter band; hairline bottom legal bar.

FORM: Four-column grid with even rhythm, hairline top rule, generous separation, minimal carding.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
