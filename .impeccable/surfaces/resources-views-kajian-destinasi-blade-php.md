## Direction contract

THESIS: The destination research dossier page must read like a quiet, authoritative field journal — typographic, calm, evidence-led — instead of a gradient-and-glass hero card stack with glowing accents and bouncing imagery.

OWN-WORLD: Flat light paper ground (#F8F9FA), deep oceanic navy ink (#0B1B2B), single natural-teal accent (#0D5C52), warm gold (#B48424) reserved for data. Epilogue display face + Be Vietnam Pro body, hairline borders, generous whitespace. No gradients, glass, glow, or gradient buttons; no kicker/eyebrow labels; no bounce or elastic easing.

STORY: Visitors should feel they are reading an institutional research brief — calm authority, cultural depth, data credibility — rather than an AI-generated marketing page.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Light paper hero without badge; display headline "Kajian Destinasi Nusantara" in Epilogue 700, clamp(40px, 5.4vw, 68px), line-height 1.05, letter-spacing -0.03em; lead paragraph at most 65 characters per line; hairline-framed map figure beside the copy; gentle fade-and-rise entrance.

FORM: Editorial split and stacked sections, hairline rules instead of card chrome where calm reads better, one spacing rhythm, typography-first.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
